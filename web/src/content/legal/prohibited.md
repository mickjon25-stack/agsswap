AgsSwap is for **farm and ranch equipment, supplies, hay and forage, and livestock**. Most things you'd find around a working place are fine to list: tractors and implements, fencing, panels and gates, trailers, tack, stock tanks, hay, sealed feed, and healthy animals. But some things are dangerous, illegal, or need a license. **Don't list the items below.** Admins will reject or remove them, and repeat or serious violations can get your account closed.

This list isn't complete. If something is illegal to sell or give away in Texas or under federal law, it's not allowed even if it's not listed here. When in doubt, leave it out, or email hello@agsswap.com and ask.

## Firearms, weapons, and explosives

- **Firearms of any kind**, including rifles, shotguns, handguns, antique guns, air guns used as firearms, receivers, frames, and gun parts kits.
- **Ammunition**, reloading powder and primers, magazines, suppressors, and bump stocks.
- **Explosives**: blasting supplies, binary exploding targets, fireworks, and stump-removal explosives.
- Switchblades, brass knuckles, and other weapons that are illegal to sell where you or the buyer live.

## Pesticides, chemicals, and fertilizers

- **Restricted-use pesticides**, and any pesticide or herbicide that requires an applicator license to buy or use. **[REVIEW]**
- **Opened, partly used, unlabeled, or repackaged** pesticides, herbicides, insecticides, rodenticides, fly baits, and dewormers.
- **Anhydrous ammonia**, **ammonium nitrate** fertilizer, and other fertilizers regulated as explosive precursors.
- Fuel and gas: gasoline, diesel, kerosene, and **propane or other gas cylinders**. Empty fuel tanks and transfer tanks must be clearly described as empty and purged.
- Refrigerants, open solvents, acids, and other hazardous chemicals in anything other than their sealed original container.
- Used motor oil, hydraulic fluid, antifreeze, and other waste fluids.

## Animal health products and food

- **Prescription and veterinary-only drugs**, including antibiotics that need a veterinarian's order, **Veterinary Feed Directive (VFD)** feeds, vaccines that require refrigeration and a cold chain, and any controlled substance. **[REVIEW]**
- Used needles, syringes, and other sharps.
- **Food for people**: raw milk, home-processed meat, eggs, honey, canned goods, and baked goods. **[REVIEW: Texas cottage food and egg-sale rules.]**
- **Opened or contaminated feed**, medicated feed, and feed that has been wet, moldy, or exposed to chemicals.

## Livestock and animals

Healthy livestock is welcome in the **Livestock** category. These are **not** allowed:

- **Sick, injured, or diseased animals**, or animals from a herd or flock under a **quarantine or hold order**.
- Animals that can't legally change hands without paperwork you don't have, such as horses without a current negative **Coggins (EIA) test**, or sheep and goats without required **scrapie** ID, where those are required. **[REVIEW: Texas Animal Health Commission rules.]**
- **Wildlife**: native game animals and birds, venomous reptiles, endangered or protected species, and animals that need a state or federal permit you can't transfer. **[REVIEW: exotic hoofstock rules.]**
- **Dogs, cats, and other pets.** Working stock dogs are not allowed in this first version. **[REVIEW]**
- Animals for fighting, and any fighting gear (for example, gaffs and spurs for cockfighting).
- Unweaned young animals offered without their mother, except bottle calves, kids, or lambs clearly described as bottle babies.
- Animal parts protected by law, such as eagle and other migratory-bird feathers.

## Seeds, plants, and hay

- **Unlabeled seed for planting** that you're selling (giveaways of saved garden seed are OK if clearly described as saved seed). **[REVIEW: Texas seed law.]**
- **Noxious or invasive plants and weed seed**, and plants under a **quarantine** (for example, citrus nursery stock in quarantine areas).
- Hay must be honestly described. Don't hide mold, weeds, or blister-beetle risk in alfalfa.

## Equipment, trailers, and vehicles

- **Stolen equipment**, livestock, trailers, or tools, or anything you don't have the right to sell or give away.
- Items with **serial numbers, VINs, or PINs removed or altered**.
- Titled trailers and vehicles **without a title** you can sign over (salvage and parts-only items must say so).
- **Catalytic converters** and other items covered by Texas metal-recycling and theft rules. **[REVIEW]**
- **Recalled products**, such as heaters, chainsaws, or ladders. Check before listing at **[CPSC.gov/Recalls](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls)**.
- Equipment with known dangerous defects (failed brakes, cracked hitch, missing PTO shields) unless it's clearly listed as **parts / salvage** and the defect is described.

## Treated wood and building leftovers **[REVIEW]**

- Used **railroad ties** and **utility poles** (creosote-treated) may be listed only for fencing or corral use and must be clearly described as used treated wood. Don't market them for gardens, raised beds, or anywhere near food or water for animals.
- Asbestos-containing materials, lead-paint debris, and moldy or flood-damaged building materials aren't allowed.

## Always-prohibited items

- Illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia; alcohol; tobacco and vape products; human prescription medicines
- **Counterfeit** or knock-off branded goods
- Anything else that's illegal to sell, give away, or own

## Not allowed as listings

- **Services instead of goods**: custom baling, shredding, fence building, hauling, stud or breeding service, boarding, day labor, or job postings. Equipment, supplies, and animals are fine to list.
- **Land, leases, or hunting leases.** **[REVIEW]**
- **Spam, fake, or misleading listings**, including stock photos, bait-and-switch prices, duplicate posts, or "contact me off-site for a better deal."
- **Ads for another business or website**, or listings that just collect contact information.
- **Trash or junk dumping**: things no one could reasonably reuse. (It's fine to mark reusable items "Headed for scrap" to get them rescued.)
- Listings that include someone else's personal information, or offensive, hateful, or adult content.
- Anything that breaks our [Terms of Service](/terms) or the law.
