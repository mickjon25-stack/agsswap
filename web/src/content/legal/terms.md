**Listing types.** You can list items as:

- **Swap:** trade for other items.
- **Sell:** for a price you set.

**Your promises when you list.** You promise that:

- You own the item or have the right to give or sell it. For livestock, that means you own the animals (and any brand on them is yours or properly transferred to you). For titled trailers and vehicles, it means you hold the title or can sign it over.

You disclose known defects, damage, age, and anything a reasonable person would want to know, such as hours on equipment, hydraulic leaks, frame damage, hay cutting and storage, and for animals: age, sex, breed, known health problems, vaccinations, and whether they're bred.
- The item isn't on our [Prohibited Items list](/prohibited) and isn't illegal to sell or give away.

**Livestock listings. [REVIEW]** If you list animals, you also promise that:

- The animals are healthy to the best of your knowledge, aren't under a quarantine or hold order, and you'll disclose any known illness, injury, or treatment withdrawal times.
- You'll follow Texas Animal Health Commission and federal animal-movement rules that apply, such as official ID or tags, health certificates, negative Coggins (EIA) tests for horses, and scrapie tags for sheep and goats, where required.
- You'll handle brand transfer and any bill of sale yourself. AgsSwap doesn't inspect brands or record ownership.
- The buyer is responsible for the animal's care, transport, and health once it leaves your place.

**Our rights.** We can refuse, edit the display of, pause, expire, or remove any listing at any time, for any reason or no reason, including after it was approved. Non-free listings may expire after about 45 days.

**Distance.** Browse shows an **approximate** distance (for example, "~12 mi") calculated from ZIP codes, not exact addresses. It's an estimate only; confirm the pickup location with the other user.

**Photos.** When you upload a photo, the app removes GPS location data stored in the image file. Still, don't photograph anything that shows your address.

**Deals.** An offer or message on AgsSwap is not a binding contract with us. Any deal is only between you and the other user.
